PHYSICS Every mechanic maps to real quantum physics
Split = Superposition
Holding SPACE puts you in two lanes at once — just like a real qubit.
Phasing = Tunneling
A split particle can slip through walls it could never climb over.
Crash = Collapse
Hit a wall while whole and the wavefunction collapses. Game over.
